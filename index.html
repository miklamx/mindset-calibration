📋 Protocol Guidelines: What to do during the 5-Minute Timer Expand / Collapse

Purpose: Mental conditioning, alignment, and focus calibration before starting work—not brainstorming or active drafting.

• Step 1: Grounding & Anchor (60s) — Close out distractions, take 3 deep breaths, and repeat gratitude aloud. Ground your nervous system to halt early urgency.

• Step 2: Active Directives (90s) — State your directive clearly to set your mental target. Instruct your mind where to focus attention once the timer ends.

• Step 3: Energy Conduit Stance (90s) — Adopt an observer mindset. Reframe work so you focus strictly on standard execution rather than stressing over immediate outcomes.

• Step 4: Journal & Close (60s) — Log your #1 priority for the day in a few words, state your closing declaration, and transition into active work when the chime sounds.

💡 Quick Guide: How to use The Playbook Click to expand / collapse

Goal: Simple rules to help you start your day focused, catch distractions early, and finish work without burning out.

The Playbook: Your Simple Rules for Staying Focused

Rule 1: Daily Focus Calibration

Where You Start: Step away from the noise and hold off on email, texts, or social media for just five minutes.

How to Do It: Take three deep breaths, state your daily focus aloud, lock in your single key deliverable for today, and let go of the pressure of the outcome.

Rule 2: Spot Your Obstacles

Where You Start: Keep an eye out for moments when you hesitate, procrastinate, or feel stuck during your work.

How to Do It: Write down what triggered you, how you felt, and what you did instead. Review these notes at the end of the week to see what's holding you back.

Rule 3: Clear Your Mind & Reset

Where You Start: When you finish a major task, meeting, or project.

How to Do It: Take a brief breath to mark the win, pause before jumping into the next thing, and leave the stress behind so you don't carry fatigue into the rest of your day.
